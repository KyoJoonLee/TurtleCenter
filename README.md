# TurtleCenter# 🐢 TurtleCenter

### Multi-AI Agent Collaboration & Orchestration Platform

**One Center. Eight AI Agents. Working Together.**

TurtleCenter is a Windows-based multi-AI agent orchestration platform developed by **Params Co., Ltd.** in South Korea.

Instead of working with a single AI assistant, TurtleCenter brings together eight specialized AI agents that communicate, coordinate tasks, and collaborate on real-world projects.

## 🚀 Key Features

- **8 Specialized AI Agents** — An architect, a coordinator, and six domain specialists.
- **Agent-to-Agent Communication** — AI agents exchange messages and coordinate work.
- **Mission Orchestration** — Assign, execute, and track collaborative tasks.
- **Persistent Context** — Maintain agent-specific knowledge and project records.
- **MCP Integration** — Connect AI agents with tools, services, and data.
- **Centralized Workspace** — Manage multiple AI agents from one Windows application.

## 💡 Our Vision

AI should not have to work alone.

We believe the future of AI is collaboration — specialized agents working together as a coordinated team.

**TurtleCenter turns individual AI assistants into an AI team.**
